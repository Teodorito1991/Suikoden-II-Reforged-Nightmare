## Difficulty and builds

Fallback factors are enemy HP1.60, damage1.45, XP1.00 and Potch0.85. Enemy/boss/hunt
profiles or role pacing can select different final factors. These do not all
multiply together. No blanket stat increase is added for publication.

The full character module intentionally changes selected builds. The earliest
prototype's unchanged-player-stats baseline is not a description of today's
80-profile module. Independently configurable layers are documented in
CONFIGURATION.md. Native legality, artwork and story callbacks remain central.

Simulations and native fixtures do not prove every party can beat every boss
without grinding. Report unavoidable status chains, excessive length or broken
scripted phases with exact version, party and settings.

## Rune charges and inns

A charge budget goes to the lowest level below its normal maximum; higher levels
wait until lower ones are full. It never exceeds normal capacity. Escape/non-victory
gets no victory regeneration. Defeated participants are excluded by default;
support/reserve rosters are not silently given recovery. The original inn routine
already fills active-party HP and normal charges, so no duplicate generic healing
patch or unverified inn feedback is added.

## War boundaries

Story-war changes use original maps, units, animations, combat and interpreter.
There is no selectable repeatable war, earned commander XP or army training.
The retired custom arena is absent from the plugin; archived pure test inputs
preserve regression coverage only.
